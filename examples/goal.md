# Synthetic local research

Compare two simple estimates on synthetic numbers. Use the configured research
Groups: Direction bounds the question; Exp plans and prepares the numerical
example in its own Group workspace, executes it once through the managed local
command service, and obtains independent Evaluate. Synthesis records the evidence
and limitations and proposes the final output publication.

Agent assignments identify their writable workspace in `work`. Keep code and
small reports there; the Group program exports selected files for handoff. Agents
are leaf specialists; the configured programs arrange independent review.

No real datasets, credentials, network shell calls or external compute are
authorized. This example demonstrates orchestration, not research benefit.
