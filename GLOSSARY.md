# Research coordination

The language used to coordinate work within a Study.

## Language

**Group**:
A capability specialized for a scope of problems, consisting of one or more experts.

**Group Definition**:
The versioned description of a Group and its capabilities available to the Router.

**Group Run**:
An independent execution of a Group for one task.

**Router**:
The coordinator that assigns work to Groups and routes their outputs and requests for help.

**Round**:
A unit of Study work ending in a published result or a retained unfinished state.
